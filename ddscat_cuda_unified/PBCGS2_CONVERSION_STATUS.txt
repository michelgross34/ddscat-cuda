PBCGS2 CUDA conversion status
==============================

Converted DDSCAT path:
  CMDSOL='PBCGS2' -> ZBCG2, L=2, NONZERO_X=.FALSE.

CUDA implementation preserves:
  - two BiCG substeps per outer iteration;
  - ZBCG2 complex conjugated dot products through chunked cublasZdotc;
  - the 3x3 Hermitian Gram matrix and convex polynomial phase;
  - omega update from Y0(L+1);
  - DELTA=1e-2 reliable residual/update logic (RCMP / XPDT);
  - final true residual computation;
  - no diagonal preconditioner, matching PRECOND in zbcg2wp.f90.

GPU residency:
  RR, R0..R2, U0..U2, XP, BP and X remain device resident.
  Green tensor, FFT work, CXADIA, CXAOFF and IOCC remain device resident.
  No cudaMemcpy H2D or D2H appears in the iterative/reliable-update loop.
  Only mapped residual/status scalars are read by the host for convergence and
  reliable-update control. The solution vector is copied D2H once at solver exit.

MATVEC:
  One persistent rank-3 cufftPlanMany(batch=3) is used for X/Y/Z components.
  Every PBCGS2 MATVEC is device-to-device.

Validation performed here:
  - CUDA/Fortran/C interface compilation with GCC/GFortran: OK.
  - Complete DDSCAT CMake/GFortran build and link: OK.
  - 3x3 convex-polynomial algebra checked against the Fortran formulas on
    1000 random complex Gram matrices: OK.

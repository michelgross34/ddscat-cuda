GPU FINAL SECTIONS CHANGE
=========================

Targets:
  ddscat_cuda
  ddscat_cuda_slice

Added:
  - GPU EVALQ for Cabs/Cext/Cpha.
  - GPU SCAT for finite-target Csca, Csca*g moments, backscatter, and F1/F2
    selected-direction amplitudes when CMDTRQ=NOTORQ.
  - fused phase/contribution CUDA kernel with double block accumulation.
  - cublasZgemv for second-level block reduction.
  - cublasDdot for angular quadrature reductions.
  - release of 13 compact Krylov vectors before final GPU postprocessing;
    lazy reallocation on the next iterative solve.

Not changed:
  - physical formulas and angular quadrature used by DDSCAT SCAT;
  - DOTORQ remains on original CPU SCAT;
  - Green/MATVEC FFT buffers remain resident for subsequent solves.

FFT note:
  Standard cuFFT samples a Cartesian k-grid, whereas DDSCAT SCAT evaluates
  spherical/arbitrary k-vectors. The exact direct phase sum is therefore used
  on GPU. A future optional NUFFT backend could accelerate very large angular
  sets without requiring a huge Cartesian interpolation grid.
